The social media problem

a plan, not just an app

SOCIAL,
SOLVED.

The biggest blocker isn't an app — it's "who posts on Instagram today?" Here's how we make that effort almost disappear.

  • Reuse what you already photograph. Every shake the prep team makes is a post. We capture them once, schedule for the week.
  • Auto-share new menu items. When admin adds an item to the menu, draft IG + FB posts get generated.
  • Surface real reviews. Charlie, Rachel, Jenevi — their 5-star Google reviews become Instagram carousels.
  • 15 minutes / week, not daily. One scheduled session a week keeps the feed alive.
